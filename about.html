Hi, I'm Franco

I'm a gameplay programmer from Buenos Aires, and I've been making games professionally since 2020. After two years of Computer Engineering, I switched to Game Development at Escuela Da Vinci, where I now teach Unity programming.

Along the way I built AR apps for Microsoft HoloLens at Cubit, worked on a Ubisoft AAA title at Globant, shipped two AI-powered mobile games at Gaxos.ai (leading the second one), and programmed core systems for Welcome Mortals, a PC tower defense made for a private client.

These days I'm focused on PC games and gameplay systems. If you're looking for a game programmer, let's talk.
