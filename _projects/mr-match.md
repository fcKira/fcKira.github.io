The jam's theme was "Connection". You play an old, eccentric cupid who shoots love bullets at pedestrians to make couples and earn points.

## What I built

- **Gameplay** and scoring.
- **Ten pedestrian types**, each with its own spawn sounds.
- **Randomized clothes and hair colors** for the basic pedestrians.

Team: Marcelo Gilardoni (game design), Carla Gilardoni and Maximiliano Garcia (2D art).
