The jam's theme was "Connection". You play an old, eccentric cupid who shoots love bullets at pedestrians to make couples and earn points. As the only programmer on the team, I wrote all of the game's code in 48 hours.

## What I built

- **Object pooling and a factory** for the ten pedestrian types, keeping spawns cheap and making new types easy to add, even under jam time pressure.
- **Randomized clothes and hair colors**, so the crowd never looks repeated.
- **Score system** that rewards each match differently depending on the type of couple formed.
- **Level timer** and the full gameplay loop, each pedestrian with its own spawn sounds.

Team: Marcelo Gilardoni (game design), Carla Gilardoni and Maximiliano Garcia (2D art).
